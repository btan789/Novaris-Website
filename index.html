STEM Racing is an international competition that merges the thrill of racing with the power of collaborative teamwork. It inspires high-school students to explore the world of STEM, providing the next generation of engineers, innovators, and problem-solvers with a pathway to future careers in STEM.

Novaris is an Australian STEM Racing Team with a history of success.
